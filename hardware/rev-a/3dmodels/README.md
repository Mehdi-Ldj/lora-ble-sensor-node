# Rev A 3D bodies

## `SMD1812P050TF-30.step` — F1

Custom dimensioned body for the RUILON `SMD1812P050TF/30` PTC fuse.

- Body envelope: **4.55 × 3.24 × 0.80 mm**
- Datasheet range: 4.37–4.73 × 3.07–3.41 mm; height 0.50–1.00 mm
- Rounded vertical edges, 0.08 mm fillet
- Colors: dark body, silver end caps
- Source drawing: PTTC SMD1812 HF series datasheet

## `MM8130-2600.step` — J6

Custom dimensioned body for the Murata `MM8130-2600RA2` coaxial switch.

- Body: **2.5 × 2.5 × 1.4 mm**
- Shield diameter: **2.1 mm**
- Center opening: **0.8 mm**
- Center contact: **0.5 mm**
- Colors: black body, silver shield, gold center contact
- Source drawing: Murata SWF series / MM8130-2600 datasheet

This is an independent mechanical envelope, not a copy of the vendor CAD. The
exact Samacsys STEP is not tracked or redistributed. If you have it under its
license, place it locally as `MM8130-2600RA2.stp`; that path is excluded by
`.gitignore`.
